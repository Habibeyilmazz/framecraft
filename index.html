With a screenshot loaded and a tool picked, press Enter to add an annotation. Arrow keys move the selected annotation (the newest, or the one picked in the Annotations list), Shift moves it further, Alt with arrow keys resizes it, Delete removes it. A new text label moves focus to its text box; type the words, then press Enter to come back. With the Pointer tool, drag the round handle on the selected annotation to resize it, or pick a colour to recolour it.
